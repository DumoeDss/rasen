## Why

The frozen-action session executor (`ecp-frozen-action-session-executor`, shipped and archived 2026-08-09, ship `65092938`) delivered the authoritative execution spine: the single `dispatchGrantedAction` contract every driver face routes through, the computed OS-by-backend capability matrix, the typed `execution-lost` mechanism at the executor's Action-outcome reconciliation, committed-frontier resume, transactional completion, the authoritative reuse/handoff/touch/retire policy source and enforcement, and the 7.1 production wiring that routes the CLI, Management API, Canvas, daemon, and launchers through that one contract with a representative two-face parity gate. Its independent review (round 1, CLEAN) and the LEAD close-out handoff explicitly routed three pieces of work here that the executor deliberately excluded so it could ship a coherent, purely-additive core: the exhaustive cross-driver same-Run parity enforcement, the exhaustive cancel/restart/ack-loss fault matrix, and the operational authoritativeness of the reuse policy across every face with a real configuration surface.

This change owns those three pieces. It consumes the executor's shipped contract, matrix, execution-lost mechanism, committed-frontier partition, and reuse-policy resolver as immutable seams and extends them to exhaustive enforcement and proof: every driver face against every operation, every named failure mode in the cancel/restart/ack-loss matrix, and a configurable, provenance-bearing policy source whose decisions are face-invariant. With this change, the session execution and control layer is operationally complete for 0.2.0 - the self-hosting toy-Change proof (acceptance 7) and the real-OS/real-backend receipts remain operator/ECP-8-owned.

## What Changes

- Deliver exhaustive cross-driver same-Run parity enforcement (slice acceptance 6's full operational truth). The executor wired every face through one contract and shipped a representative two-face parity gate; this change adds an exhaustive parity harness that proves EVERY driver face (Claude/Codex interactive launcher, bare CLI, Management API, Canvas, Operations/audit plane, daemon) routes EVERY operation (start, resume, cancel, inspect, audit) through the shared projector/control contract to the same canonical Run/Action, with the capability matrix as the sole "when capability allows" oracle. It adds the audit operation to the parity surface, proves the headless-driver-independent-of-launcher property per face, and installs a drift-prevention gate so a face that develops a divergent Run/Session truth fails closed rather than silently regressing.
- Deliver the exhaustive cancel/restart/ack-loss fault matrix (slice acceptance 4). The executor shipped the `execution-lost` mechanism plus committed-frontier resume and representative receipts; this change builds the full fault-injection matrix over every named failure mode - cancel-before-start, cancel-in-flight, host/daemon restart, worker process loss, completion ack loss, duplicate completion, and stale control - using the executor's injectable backend seams as the deterministic fault-injection substrate. Each matrix entry proves recovery continues ONLY the uncommitted frontier, already-committed invocations and effects are not re-executed, and any state that cannot be proven is typed-waited or escalated (fail-closed), never silently completed or silently dropped.
- Make session reuse/handoff/touch/retire policy operationally authoritative across all driver faces with a real configuration surface (slice acceptance 5). The executor's resolver ships numeric limits at `default` provenance because, in its own documented words, "there is no authoring surface for the numeric limits yet" (`src/core/frozen-action-executor/reuse-policy.ts`); this change adds the operator/author configuration surface that supplies the executor policy block so `handoffTokenLimit`, `reuseRoundLimit`, and the touch/retire cadence can carry `authored` or `definition` provenance instead of always `default`. It proves the resolved policy and the reuse/handoff/retire decision are face-invariant - the same regardless of which driver face asks - and that every enforced value traces end-to-end to a configured source and provenance.
- Keep the executor's contract, matrix, execution-lost mechanism, committed-frontier partition, transactional completion, and reuse-policy resolver unmodified. This change consumes them; it does not redesign them. It adds no signing or key-custody (decision 12) and does not touch `native/**`, the archived changes, or the operator-owned self-hosting seam.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `session-policy-and-control-parity`: exhaustive cross-driver same-Run parity enforcement across every face and operation (including the audit plane) with a drift-prevention gate; the exhaustive cancel/restart/ack-loss fault-injection matrix proving exactly-once, fail-closed, committed-frontier-only recovery under every named failure mode; and the configurable, provenance-bearing, face-invariant reuse/handoff/touch/retire policy source.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The executor capability (`frozen-action-session-executor`), the durable agent session host capabilities (`durable-agent-session-host`, `session-supervision`, `daemon-residency`), `ecp-change-run-runtime`, and the runtime/adapter registry are consumed as immutable seams. The executor's reuse requirement already states the policy block carries `authored | definition | default` provenance; this change supplies the configuration surface that produces the non-`default` values and proves face-invariance, so no existing requirement heading is renamed and no scenario is implicitly deleted. Cross-child worker reuse (`worker-reuse-config`, `worker-reuse-orchestration`) remains out of scope.

## Impact

- Primary code surfaces: a new parity-enforcement harness and drift-prevention gate over the shipped `dispatchGrantedAction` contract and daemon endpoint (`src/core/management-api/frozen-action-executor.ts`); a new exhaustive fault-injection matrix built on the executor's injectable `HostedBackendSeam` / `InToolBackendSeam` (`src/core/frozen-action-executor/executor.ts`) and its committed-frontier partition (`action-outcome.ts`); the audit operation wired into the Operations/projector surface; and a new policy-configuration source that supplies the `ExecutorPolicyBlock` to `resolveReusePolicy` (`reuse-policy.ts`) with provenance, resolved through the existing configuration chain. - The frozen authority crates, the legacy ProcessCapsule, the durable session-host registry record shape, and the executor's transactional-completion path are not modified. The daemon endpoint continues to perform no Record mutation; completion stays the canonical Facade path.
- DAG: this change depends on `ecp-frozen-action-session-executor` (archived); it blocks `ecp-session-self-hosting-vertical-proof` (operator-owned). It adds no other edges and does not touch the parked provider changes, the archived changes, or the self-hosting seam.
- Governing decisions: locked decision 11 (execution-lost and committed-frontier resume - consumed, not re-derived), locked decision 12 (threat model is our own mistakes; no signing/key-custody; fail-closed typed uncertainty and actor separation preserved), locked decision 13 (best-effort backend roster - consumed via the matrix).
- Direction source: `rasen/work/issue-centered-automation-platform/executable-composite-pipelines/slices/session-execution-and-self-hosting/` (slice spec acceptance 4, 5, 6; the executor's review item 6 and the LEAD close-out handoff route them here).
