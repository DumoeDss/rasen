## One byte-level note worth keeping

The synced spec keeps a trailing EOF blank line. `archive.json` records
`specSync.afterSha256 = 6de00eba...`, which is the sha256 of exactly those
bytes, so trimming it to satisfy a whitespace lint would desync a
content-addressed record. (The opposite call was correct in the rasen repo,
where archive receipts do NOT digest canonical specs and CI DOES gate
whitespace.) On the store's main checkout the same file hashes differently in
the WORKING TREE purely because autocrlf smudges it to CRLF; the git blob is
identical (00b32655...) and its content hashes to the recorded value.
